Common questions

Do my guests need an app?

No. The page opens in their phone’s browser. iPhone XS and newer read the tag without an app. Android reads it when NFC is on and the screen is unlocked. On Android 17 and newer, a notification appears, and they tap it to open the link.

What if their phone doesn’t read the tag?

They scan the QR code next to it and get the same page. iPhone won’t read the tag while the Camera or Wallet app is open, or in Airplane Mode.

Can I change the Wi-Fi password or house rules later?

Yes. Send us the change on WhatsApp, and we update it remotely, with no visit to the unit. The tag itself never changes.

Who installs it, and where?

We install it ourselves, currently in Jeddah only. Installation is included, and we test every tag before handover.

Will guests see my number, or my housekeeper’s?

Never. Check-out and maintenance messages come to us, from the guest’s phone. Your number isn’t on the page or in the message.

How does renewal work?

It renews only with your explicit confirmation. We remind you 7 days before renewal on monthly billing, and 30 days before on yearly.

What’s in the trial, and do I pay anything upfront?

We set up the guide and install the hardware in one unit, and you try it free for two weeks. No upfront payment and no commitment during the trial. If you don’t continue, we pick up the hardware.

What if a tag stops working?

We respond to your report within an hour, 9 AM⁠–⁠11 PM, every day, and replace the tag free within 5 business days, as long as the fault isn’t from misuse.

Want to talk to a person?

Someone on our team replies within an hour, 9 AM⁠–⁠11 PM, every day.
